# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be polite, inclusive, and professional.
- Focus on constructive feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, or unprofessional conduct will not be tolerated.
